Our School

A Culture of Excellence and Integrity

Since our establishment in 1965, we have remained committed to providing quality education in a disciplined and supportive learning environment. Education at Kigumo Bendera goes beyond academic achievement by nurturing integrity, leadership, innovation, and responsibility.

Through the dedication of our teachers, support from parents, and determination of our students, we continue to build a culture that prepares young people to become confident, responsible, and successful members of society.

  • Dedicated and experienced teaching staff
  • Disciplined, safe and supportive campus
  • Holistic development through clubs and sports
